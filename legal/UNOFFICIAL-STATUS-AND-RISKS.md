Image development and offline profile planning are distinct from camera writes. A camera-profile upload changes camera settings and can fail after a write has started. A saved backup is not proof of a tested restore operation. Confirm the exact camera, bank, family and slot; preserve originals; inspect saved results before retrying an uncertain upload. This tool does not provide an official firmware-update facility or a complete HSL-camera uploader. Stock profile sharing can reset H/L values.
