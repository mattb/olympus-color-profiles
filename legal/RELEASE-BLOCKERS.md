# Unresolved publication gates

The owner selected MIT for owned original code/documentation. That choice is not a clearance of every source file, artistic asset, vendor agreement or generated image. The maintainer authorized publication of the curated source on 2026-11-15. That publication decision does not resolve the legal or qualification questions below.

| Gate | Status / required evidence |
|---|---|
| OM Workspace private-interface and research methods | Unresolved. The [official agreement](https://download.omsystem.com/pages/owdownload/en/goLicence.html) restricts reverse engineering, modification and vendor-software distribution. Applicable terms, jurisdiction and conditioned interoperability exceptions require a documented assessment. Disclaimers and source-only distribution are not a complete answer. |
| Original-source provenance | The maintainer confirmed on 2026-11-15 that no implementation was copied from external projects. This records firsthand authorship information, not an independently certified clean-room audit. Retain contributor ownership records and separate third-party artistic/vendor rights; research citations alone do not imply copied implementation. |
| Community camera recipes | Three forum-derived COLOR XMPs and historical reports containing their mappings are excluded from the curated candidate. Permission, a documented rights assessment or continued exclusion remains necessary. This does not presume every numerical setting is copyrightable. |
| Artistic mappings | Identified RawTherapee adaptations retain CC BY-SA attribution/license/change notices, including artistic mappings embedded in reports. Independent code is not automatically CC licensed. |
| Vendor assets and media | No vendor app/framework/helper/shader, staged executable, firmware, manual, camera backup or personal image belongs in this source candidate. Embedded ICC profiles in future example media require their own rights review. |
| Adobe external-tool boundary | The maintainer accepts retaining the optional, separately installed local Converter integration. [LRTimelapse's installation guide](https://lrtimelapse.com/install/) and [EULA section 9](https://lrtimelapse.com/eula/) provide explicit external-tool precedent. Converter's own terms still apply; no Adobe component is bundled or relicensed. Bundling, application modification or hosted-service claims would need their own assessment; special Adobe permission is not being imposed as a new ordinary-local-use gate. |
| Fresh Mac | Still outstanding. Isolated exports on a provisioned Mac are not a genuine fresh-machine installation test. See [release readiness](../docs/release-readiness.md). |
| Public history | Original private history and evidence are preserved. The curated candidate omits them; this is not a certificate that publishing the original Git history is safe. |

Research details are retained in the private development repository. Public-source disclosures are in [third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md), [license status](LICENSE-STATUS.md), and [unofficial status](UNOFFICIAL-STATUS-AND-RISKS.md). These are project release gates, not legal advice or a claim that a vendor must approve every interoperability project.

The [upstream precedent review](../docs/developers/upstream-licensing-precedents.md) documents the HSL firmware project's licensing boundaries and its separate firmware-containing releases. Useful upstream hygiene and public availability do not establish a vendor waiver or resolve this project's Workspace-specific questions.
